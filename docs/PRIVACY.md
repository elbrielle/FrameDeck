# FrameDeck privacy policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026.

FrameDeck rotates the browser tabs you choose. It does not require an account and has no advertising, analytics, telemetry, or developer-operated server.

## Information used on your device

FrameDeck reads the titles and URLs of open tabs so you can choose and arrange a deck. It uses tab and window identifiers to switch between those tabs. When you save a deck, its name, ordered tab URLs, durations, and display preferences are saved in Chrome's local extension storage. General preferences are also stored locally. Rotation state is kept for the current browser session.

This information is used only to display, save, restore, and rotate your chosen tabs. FrameDeck does not send it to the developer or other parties, sell it, or use it for advertising. It does not read your browser history, page text, form entries, passwords, or cookies.

## Optional page effects

When you enable page effects and grant website access, FrameDeck can add its own countdown and transition elements to supported pages in your deck. These elements display timing information. They do not copy or transmit page content. You can revoke website access through Chrome's extension controls.

## Storage and deletion

Saved decks and preferences remain in the Chrome profile where you installed FrameDeck until you change or remove them. FrameDeck does not use Chrome's sync storage. Delete a saved deck from FrameDeck to remove that saved configuration. Uninstalling FrameDeck removes its extension storage. Session rotation information does not continue into a new browser session.

Stored URLs may contain private information, including query parameters. Save only the tabs you want retained on this device. FrameDeck's local extension storage is not an encrypted vault.

## Websites you open

Restoring a saved deck can reopen its websites. Those websites make their own network requests and handle information under their own privacy policies, just as they do when you open them directly. FrameDeck does not control those websites.

## Contact and changes

For privacy questions, use the developer contact listed on FrameDeck's Chrome Web Store listing. General support is available at https://github.com/elbrielle/FrameDeck/issues. Do not post private tab URLs or other sensitive information in public issues. This policy will be updated if FrameDeck's data handling changes.
